1996 Conterminous U.S. Hazard Maps Seismicity Catalogs

Seismicity catalogs used in the seismic hazard model are available. The western U.S. (WUS) catalog is in moment magnitude (Mw) and the central and eastern U.S. (CEUS) catalog is in body wave magnitude (mb). For more information on the compilation of the seismicity catalogs, please see the USGS Open-File Report 96-532 (https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1996/532/).

Catalog files are presented in text format.

Western U.S. Catalog (wuscat.txt)
Central and Eastern U.S. Catalog (ceuscat.txt)

File Format:

Column 1: Magnitude (Mw for WUS and mb for CEUS)
Column 2: Longitude (degrees, west)
Column 3: Latitude (degrees, north)
Column 4: Depth (km)
Column 5: Year
Column 6: Month
Column 7: Day
Column 8: Hour
Column 9: Minute
Column 10: Second
Column 11: Source Catalog
